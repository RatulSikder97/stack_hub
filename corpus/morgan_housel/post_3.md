# The story always wins

A spreadsheet has never changed anyone's mind by itself. The number convinces almost no one. The story attached to the number convinces nearly everyone.

This is one of the most underrated facts in business. People who spend their careers obsessing over data accuracy often lose to people who pair an okay number with a great explanation. The explanation does not have to be deceptive. Most often it is not. It is just better at packaging the same information into a shape the human brain accepts.

Markets work the same way. A good quarter from a company without a story is a footnote. A good quarter from a company with a story about the future the world is moving toward becomes a multi-year rerating.

The cautionary version of this is that stories also fail in shapes the data did not predict. When the narrative breaks, the same numbers that supported the rise stop being able to support the fall. People who only watched the numbers cannot understand what just happened. People who watched the story can.

If you are deciding anything important, I would not throw the spreadsheet away. But I would notice which story the spreadsheet is currently being asked to serve, and whether the story has changed under your feet.
