# Doing things badly on purpose

Perfectionism, in my experience, is rarely about wanting things to be perfect. It is about wanting to avoid the small, public moment in which something you made is judged and found ordinary.

The fix is not to lower standards in some general way. It is to deliberately do something badly on purpose, in front of someone, and discover that the world continues. This is hard, because most of us spent our school years being rewarded for doing the safe version well, rather than the risky version at all.

I have a small ritual that helps. I write the first version of any post in a doc I never plan to publish, and I let it be terrible. I do not edit it. I just notice that the terrible version exists, and that the world has not ended. Then I write the version I will publish, with the pressure already drained out of the question of whether I am capable.

The willingness to make something ordinary is, paradoxically, the only path I have ever found to making anything not ordinary.
