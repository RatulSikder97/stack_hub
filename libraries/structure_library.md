# Structure Library

## The Concept Lens
- **Scaffold:** Open with symptom -> name hidden principle -> explain mechanism -> apply to 2 contexts -> close with one operating rule.
- **When to use:** One clear concept needs practical translation.
- **When NOT to use:** Story-heavy or personal confession pieces.
- **Warning:** Do not define terms for too long before showing consequences.

## The Three Examples
- **Scaffold:** Thesis -> example 1 (simple) -> example 2 (counter-case) -> example 3 (high-stakes) -> synthesis -> action.
- **When to use:** Readers need pattern recognition through contrast.
- **When NOT to use:** When you only have one strong example.
- **Warning:** Keep each example structurally distinct; avoid repetitive rhythm.

## The Reversal
- **Scaffold:** State common belief -> show where it fails -> offer reverse framing -> prove with evidence -> implementation steps.
- **When to use:** High-value myth-busting or reframing topics.
- **When NOT to use:** Nuanced topics where reversal would over-simplify.
- **Warning:** Do not caricature the original view just to make reversal easier.

## The Personal Investigation
- **Scaffold:** Trigger event -> hypothesis -> test/process -> results -> revised belief -> reader adaptation.
- **When to use:** You have credible first-hand experimentation.
- **When NOT to use:** You cannot provide concrete observations.
- **Warning:** Keep ego low; story serves the idea, not the narrator.

## The Listicle Done Right
- **Scaffold:** One-line promise -> numbered items with varied depth and example type -> synthesis paragraph -> screenshot-able close.
- **When to use:** Tactical posts with clear discrete takeaways.
- **When NOT to use:** Complex argument requiring narrative build-up.
- **Warning:** Vary length, tone, and example type across items. Sameness is why most listicles fail.

## The Letter
- **Scaffold:** Direct address -> shared tension -> hard truth -> compassionate challenge -> concrete invitation.
- **When to use:** Relationship-building or vulnerable positioning moments.
- **When NOT to use:** Weekly default format or highly technical topics.
- **Warning:** Use sparingly — once per quarter max.
