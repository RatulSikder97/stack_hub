# Phase 4 Monetization Roadmap

This is strategy, not infrastructure. Build the infrastructure when the trust account check passes.

## 1) Trust account check
- 3+ months consistent free publishing on cadence
- Open rate >40% on list >1,000
- Share-to-open ratio >2%
- 3+ posts that generated unsolicited reader replies
- Clear answer for what stays free

## 2) Welcome email strategy
Use one hook pattern in subject, link best 3 archive posts, promise the next specific post.

## 3) Dual CTA strategy
Soft top CTA for new readers; specific bottom CTA for next delivery cadence; reassess at 5,000+ subscribers.

## 4) Pricing
Monthly base, annual >=30% cheaper, founding member ~50% above standard for 14 days.

## 5) Free vs paid
Free must be longer/more valuable than paid at launch; lead with what remains free.

## 6) Launch sequence
Day 0: launch announcement (Personal Investigation). Day 7: founding reminder. Day 14: founding tier closes.

## 7) Do-not-launch conditions
Do not launch if trust account check fails, cadence breaks, or free value proposition is unclear.
