You are not overwhelmed because life is unusually hard. You are overwhelmed because too many decisions remain unresolved.

## What is actually draining you?
Unmade decisions act like background tabs in the mind. Each one costs a little attention every hour.

## How to recover control
Pick one delayed decision, define the next irreversible step, and commit to it today.

The calm you want is mostly a byproduct of fewer open loops.
