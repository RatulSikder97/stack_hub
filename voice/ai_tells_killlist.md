# AI Tells Kill List

1. "It's not just X, it's Y" structures — overused synthetic contrast pattern.
2. "In a world where..." openings — generic and cinematic filler.
3. Em-dashes as stylistic flourish — now a common AI signature.
4. "Let's explore..." / "Let's dive into..." — assistant-like framing, weak authority.
5. Three-item lists where two would do — unnecessary symmetry reads machine-made.
6. Closing with "The choice is yours" or empty agency-handoff — weak, generic ending.

Any draft containing these goes back to revision. No exceptions.
